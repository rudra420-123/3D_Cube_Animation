# 🎬 3D Cube Animation Website – A Tribute to Thalapathy Vijay

Welcome to the **3D Cube Animation Website**, a creative tribute to the legendary Tamil actor **Thalapathy Vijay**. This website showcases a 3D rotating cube, each face displaying a different iconic image of Vijay sir, honoring his contribution to Indian cinema.

## 🔥 About the Website

- Built using **HTML**, **CSS (3D Transforms)**, and responsive web techniques.
- Features a **3D animated cube** displaying six iconic movie stills of Thalapathy Vijay.
- Designed to honor his legacy and present a quick visual of his cinematic journey.
- Minimal and fast-loading design with aesthetic layout.

---

## 👑 About Thalapathy Vijay

**Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar**, popularly known as **Thalapathy Vijay**, is a celebrated Indian actor, playback singer, philanthropist, and producer who predominantly works in Tamil cinema. Known for his powerful screen presence, versatile acting, and box-office dominance, Vijay is one of the highest-paid actors in South India.

### 📌 Quick Bio

- **Full Name:** Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar  
- **Born:** June 22, 1974, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India  
- **Parents:** S. A. Chandrasekhar (director), Shoba Chandrasekhar (playback singer)  
- **Debut:** 1984 (as a child artist in *Vetri*)  
- **Lead Debut:** 1992 (*Naalaiya Theerpu*)  
- **Nickname:** Ilayathalapathy (Young Commander), Thalapathy (Commander)  
- **Fanbase:** Massive and global  

### 🎖️ Achievements

- Multiple **Tamil Nadu State Film Awards** and **Vijay Awards**
- Several box-office records across India and overseas
- Known for his philanthropic activities, including education and healthcare support for the underprivileged
- Known for his inspirational speeches and social responsibility
